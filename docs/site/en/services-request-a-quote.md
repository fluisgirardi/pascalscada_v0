##### What helps us quote

You do not need every answer; whatever you know already helps.

* **Kind of work**: supervisory application, driver or integration, support, review, migration or training.
* **Process**: the industry and what the application will supervise (a machine, a line, a plant).
* **Equipment**: makes and models of the PLCs, drives and instruments, and the protocols if you know them.
* **Size**: approximate number of screens and tags (variables).
* **Features**: alarms, recipes, history, reports, users, integration with other systems.
* **Where it will run**: Windows or Linux, PC, panel PC or ARM.
* Desired **schedule** and, if you have one, a **budget range**.
* **Country** and preferred **language** (English or Portuguese).

Proposals are made in US dollars (USD). The data you send is only used to answer your request.
