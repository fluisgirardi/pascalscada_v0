##### Support and training

For teams that build their own applications with PascalSCADA and want to get there faster, or with more confidence.

##### Technical support

A block of hours for your team to use as needed: questions about using the library, diagnosing communication problems, analysing network captures (Wireshark), code review. Support is by e-mail and online meetings, and the hours used are reported after each request.

##### Project review

An analysis of your application, before start-up or when it has problems: architecture, use of tags and drivers, scan times, performance, handling of communication faults. You receive a report with what was found and what to fix.

##### Application migration

* From old PascalSCADA versions or Delphi to the current Lazarus.
* From Windows to Linux, or to ARM Linux.
* From another supervisory system to PascalSCADA.

##### Training

Online training, for one person or a team, built around what you are going to develop: installation, communication ports, drivers and tags, screens and controls, alarms, recipes, security and databases. In English or Portuguese.
