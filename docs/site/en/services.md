##### Supervisory application development

PascalSCADA is open source and free, and it stays that way. If you would rather **have the application built** than build it yourself, the author of the library develops supervisory applications, drivers and integrations on demand, for customers in Brazil and abroad.

| | |
|---|---|
| No per-tag or runtime licences | The application you receive is yours: install it on as many machines as you like, with no cost per tag, screen or station. |
| Source code included | You get the complete Lazarus project and can maintain it, extend it or hire someone else to do so. |
| Cross-platform | Windows, Linux (ARM included: Raspberry Pi, industrial panel PCs) and FreeBSD, from the same code. |
| Built by the author of the drivers | Communication problems are fixed where they start, in the driver, not worked around in the application. |

##### What you can hire

* **[Supervisory applications](/services-supervisory-applications/)**: a complete HMI/SCADA with screens, alarms, recipes, history, users and reports.
* **[Protocol drivers and integrations](/services-protocol-drivers/)**: a protocol PascalSCADA does not speak yet, or connecting the plant to OPC UA, MQTT, databases and MES/ERP systems.
* **[Support and training](/services-support-and-training/)**: technical support hours for your team, project reviews, application migration and online training.

##### How it works

1. **Briefing**: you describe the project in the [quote request](/services-request-a-quote/).
2. **Call**: an online meeting to understand the process, the equipment and what you expect.
3. **Proposal**: scope, delivery milestones, schedule and price in writing.
4. **Development**: delivered by milestone, each one for you to test.
5. **Testing and commissioning**: bench testing (simulator or test PLC) and support during the plant start-up, remote or on site.
6. **Warranty and support**: fixes within the warranty period of the proposal and, if you want, ongoing support after it.

##### Pricing

All work is **quoted per project**: every application depends on the process, the equipment and the scope, so the price is given after the briefing and the call. Proposals are made in **US dollars (USD)**.
